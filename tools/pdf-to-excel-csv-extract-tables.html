PDF → Excel/CSV (Extract Tables)

Extracts embedded text from a PDF and formats it into rows suitable for CSV/Excel.

Best for

  • Digitally generated PDFs (exports from Excel/BI tools)
  • Simple tables with consistent row alignment

Not ideal for

  • Scanned PDFs (use OCR first)
  • Very complex multi-column layouts
