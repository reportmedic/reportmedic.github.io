Exam Boards

What each assessment actually measures, how long you get, and the single thing that most often separates the top band from the middle in that system.

Specifications change. Set texts move between papers, assessment weightings are revised, and a novel on the list one year may not be the next. Everything here describes the shape and the criteria of each assessment, which is stable, rather than which paper this novel currently sits on, which is not. Check the current specification and your teacher before planning a revision timetable around any of it.
