The Periods

The conventional framework, what defines each period, and where the dates are firm against where they are argued over.

All dates before roughly 690 BC are approximate and are debated between the high, middle and low chronologies, with the disagreement widening the further back you go. Different textbooks give different dates for the same reign and neither is wrong. Say which chronology you are following when precision matters.
