How this tool works

Local-only: your PDF never leaves your device. We use a well-known open-source PDF parser in your browser.

  • Best for PDFs with selectable text
  • Scanned/image-only PDFs may extract little to nothing (OCR needed)
  • Layout-heavy PDFs may need small cleanup after conversion

If you want page-by-page output, enable separators/headings before converting.
