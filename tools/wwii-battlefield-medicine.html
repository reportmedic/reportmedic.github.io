WWII Battlefield Medicine Learn more

A historical reference on how armies, navies, air forces, and civilian services of the Second World War understood, treated, and were defeated by injury and disease. Twenty subject areas, each written for readers arriving from a history article and wanting the medical fact behind the sentence they just read.

This page documents medical practice as it stood in the nineteen thirties and forties, including methods long since abandoned. It is a history reference and nothing on it is clinical guidance. For any present day medical question, consult a qualified clinician.

WWII Battlefield Medicine

What this covers

  • Twenty subject areas across land, sea, air, and civilian medicine
  • How each condition was recognised and treated with the means available at the time
  • What each cost armies operationally, in casualties and in lost fighting strength
  • The innovations the war forced, and the practices it discredited

How to use it

  • Every subject has a permanent anchor for deep linking from an article
  • Search filters all twenty subjects at once by keyword
  • Filter by theatre to see what dominated a given front

What it is not

  • Not clinical guidance, and not a description of modern treatment
  • Several methods described here were abandoned for good reason
