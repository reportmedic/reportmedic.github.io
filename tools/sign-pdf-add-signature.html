Sign PDF

What this does

  • Lets you draw a signature and stamp it onto a PDF page.
  • Exports a new PDF locally in your browser.

Good to know

  • Encrypted / password-protected PDFs can’t be signed here yet. Use the “PDF Password Protect / Unlock” tool first.
  • The signature is a visual stamp (like signing a printed page), not a cryptographic certificate signature.
