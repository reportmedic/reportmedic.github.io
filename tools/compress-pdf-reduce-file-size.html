Compress PDF

What this helps with

  • Email attachments that exceed limits
  • Portals that reject “too large” PDFs
  • Oversized scanned documents

How it works

  • We re-render each page and rebuild a new PDF with a smaller image payload.
  • Light/Balanced/Strong control resolution and JPEG quality.

Tradeoff

  • Because pages are re-rendered, text may not remain selectable.
  • For “no quality loss” operations, use the Organizer tool.
