Phrase Occurrence Counter

What this helps with

  • Check repeated phrases in a report, email thread, transcript, or notes
  • Measure how often key terms show up in a CSV export
  • Compare wording consistency across drafts

How it works

  • You provide phrases and text (upload or paste)
  • We count occurrences for each phrase and summarize the results
  • Charts and metric cards make patterns obvious at a glance

Privacy

  • Your file stays on your device
  • No uploads, no server processing

Tips

  • Use Whole word for words like fee, plan, or due
  • Use Case sensitive if capitalization matters
  • Overlapping matches can help with patterns like AAAA containing AAA twice
