The Ten Axes

Each axis is a real variable across the period, with its possible values, what a difference along it lets you argue, and the mistake it invites. Choose the axis before choosing the novels and the comparison writes itself.

A comparison organised by theme produces a list of similarities. A comparison organised by axis produces an argument, because an axis has values and two novels sitting at different points on it are disagreeing about something. Pick the axis where your two novels differ most sharply and the essay has a spine before you write a word.
