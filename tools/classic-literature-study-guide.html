Essay Method

Eight moves that separate a top band literature essay from a competent summary.

One rule underlies all eight. A literature essay is judged on argument, not on coverage. An answer that makes one contestable claim and defends it against the strongest objection will beat an answer that mentions three times as much and asserts nothing anyone could disagree with.
