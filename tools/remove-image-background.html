Remove Image Background

Removes a solid background (white studio, flat color wall) and outputs a transparent PNG.

How it works

  • Choose a background color (or auto-pick from the corners)
  • Increase tolerance until the background disappears
  • Use feather to soften the edge

When it won’t be perfect

  • Busy backgrounds, hair, glass, or complex edges
  • Strong shadows or gradients
