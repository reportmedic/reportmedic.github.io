Heat and Body

Seven things that decide whether you finish the weekend. Read them once before you go rather than looking them up when someone is already in trouble.

The line that matters most. Heat exhaustion is recoverable with shade, rest, fluids and cooling. If a person becomes confused, stops sweating, has hot dry skin, faints, or does not improve quickly, that is a different and far more serious condition and it needs festival medical staff immediately. Do not wait and do not move them far unaided.

My Plan

The details that matter when a phone dies. Fill these in, export it, and keep a paper copy in a pocket.

Write the essentials on paper as well as in this tool. A phone that has died at eleven at night is the exact moment you need your accommodation address and one emergency number, and it is the exact moment you cannot reach them on the phone.
