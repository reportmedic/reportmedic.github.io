Image Resize / Compress

  • Upload one or more JPG/PNG/WebP images (drag-and-drop or browse).
  • Set new width/height (or keep aspect ratio and change just one).
  • Choose output format and quality (JPEG/WebP use quality; PNG is lossless).
  • Process all images in one click, then download individually or as a batch.
  • Everything runs locally in your browser - nothing is uploaded to any server.
If you're resizing for tickets/email, 1600px wide is usually plenty.
