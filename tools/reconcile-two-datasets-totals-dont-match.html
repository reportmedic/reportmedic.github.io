Reconciliation

What this helps with

  • Match two datasets by key
  • Quantify variances (amounts, counts)
  • List unmatched/missing records
  • Produce a reconciliation summary

Common causes

  • Two sources should agree but don’t
  • Missing keys, duplicates, or join issues
  • Timing differences between extracts

What to do next

  • Reconcile after you’ve identified the right keys
  • Use Cleaner to remove duplicates/noise first
  • Export the reconciliation for stakeholders
  • If you need guidance, use Fix My Report
