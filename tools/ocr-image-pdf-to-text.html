PDF Organizer

What this helps with

  • Combine multiple PDFs into one file (in the right order)
  • Extract only the pages you need for upload portals
  • Reorder pages before you send or archive

Good to know

  • Everything runs locally in your browser — files are not uploaded.
  • Reorder keeps the original pages (no quality loss).
  • Splitting can export a single PDF or a ZIP of multiple PDFs.

What to do next

  • If you’re working with scans or huge files, try “Compress PDF” afterwards.
  • If a portal rejects your document, split to isolate the failing pages.
