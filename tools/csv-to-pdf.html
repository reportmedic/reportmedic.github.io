CSV to PDF

What this helps with

  • Share a clean snapshot of CSV or TSV exports
  • Turn wide tables into a readable PDF for email or reviews
  • Print or archive a lightweight table view with a timestamp

How it works

  • You upload a CSV, TSV, or delimiter separated TXT file
  • The tool renders a table preview, then prints it to PDF
  • You can adjust paper size, orientation, and scale for wide files

Privacy

  • Your file stays on your device
  • No uploads, no server processing

Tips

  • If columns look cramped, switch to Landscape and reduce Scale
  • If parsing looks wrong, re export as CSV (UTF-8) from Excel
