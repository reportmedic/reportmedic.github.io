Gap Priority Matrix

Not every unknown matters equally. This ranks what you do not know by how much it would change your conclusion if you learned it.

A gap is worth collecting on when three things hold at once: you have low confidence in it, it would materially move your assessment, and it is realistically observable from sources you can reach. An unknown that fails the third test is not a collection priority, it is a caveat you must state and live with. The matrix below computes priority from the scores you have already entered.
