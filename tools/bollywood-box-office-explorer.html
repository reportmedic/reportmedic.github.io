Convert between the figures different sources report, and see what a headline number implies about tickets sold and money reaching the producer.

This tool contains no live collection data and reports no figures for any film. You supply the numbers from whichever tracker you use, and it does the arithmetic the trackers do not show. Indian box office figures are not independently audited the way studio reporting is elsewhere, so treat every input as an estimate rather than an accounting record.

Why Trackers Disagree

Eight reasons two reputable sources will publish different totals for the same film on the same day.

One of the largest Indian trackers states on its own pages that its figures are compiled from various sources and its own research, that they may be approximate or differ substantially from producer figures, and that it makes no claim about their authenticity. That disclaimer is honest and it applies to every source in this field. Quote a number with the tracker's name attached, never as a bare fact.
