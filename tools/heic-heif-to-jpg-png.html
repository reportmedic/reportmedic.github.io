HEIC → JPG/PNG

This tool converts HEIC/HEIF photos (common on iPhone) into JPG or PNG.

Good for

  • Sharing photos to apps that don’t accept HEIC
  • Batch converting many files at once
  • Keeping everything local (no uploads)

Notes

  • JPG is smaller; PNG is better for graphics/screenshots.
  • If a file fails, it may be an unsupported HEIC variant—try PNG or re-export from Photos.
