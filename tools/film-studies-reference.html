Film Studies Reference

What this covers

  • Technical vocabulary organised by the department that uses it
  • Movements and national cinemas, and what each was reacting against
  • The production pipeline, and who holds the decision at each stage
  • Critical terms that are used loosely, defined precisely
  • Formats, gauges, aspect ratios, and why a frame is the shape it is
  • How to cite a film, a scene, and a version in an essay

How to use it

  • Every subject has a permanent anchor for deep linking from an article
  • The search box filters all fourteen sections and the glossary at once
  • Filter by area to see only one department or one field

What it is not

  • Not a list of films, directors, or rankings, all of which date immediately
  • Not a substitute for watching. It is the vocabulary you need afterwards
