UPI QR Generator + Scanner

What this helps with

  • Create a payment QR for a UPI ID (VPA)
  • Optionally add amount, note, and reference
  • Decode a QR from an image to see the embedded link

How it works

  • We build a UPI deep link in the common upi://pay format
  • We generate the QR locally in your browser
  • For decoding, we read pixels from your image and extract the QR text

Privacy

  • Your data stays on your device
  • No uploads, no server processing
