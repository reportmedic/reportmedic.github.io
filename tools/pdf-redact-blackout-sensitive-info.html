PDF Redact (Blackout)

This tool lets you black out sensitive info in a PDF (names, IDs, account numbers) and export a new file.

How to use

  • Upload a PDF
  • Select a page, then drag on the preview to draw blackout rectangles
  • Export a new PDF (redactions are burned into the pages)

Important

  • This burns in black boxes. It does not attempt to remove underlying text objects.
  • For high-stakes redaction, always double-check the exported PDF.
